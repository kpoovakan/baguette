Privacy Policy

This is an informational disclosure. This privacy policy is not a legally binding document. Baguette's developer is most obviously a programmer, not a lawyer.

Information We Collect

When you log in to Baguette, we use GitHub OAuth to automatically collect your GitHub username. While we do have access to additional GitHub account data, Baguette does not collect other account data.

When you use Baguette, you might manually provide information to us, like the names of your bakery goods or your customer names. You can choose not to provide such information, but if you do so, it defeats the purpose of Baguette.

Our third-party services might also collect data. For more information, see Third-Party Services.

Data Use And Access

We use your data and information for the sole purpose of providing this service (Baguette) to you. We do not sell it to marketing or advertising agencies. We may also use your data and information to prevent abuse or cybercrime if necessary.

The developer and creator of Baguette (kpoovakan) is physically able to view your data at any time. We reserve the right to suspend and/or delete your data at any time without prior notice.

Third-party services may access some of your data.

Should we receive an official request from law enforcement or cybercrime authorities, we may share your data and information with them to comply with legal laws.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on a remote clouddata server. Baguette is a small project created by a single developer; your data is not encrypted nor is it stored in a fancy database. Never provide sensitive information to Baguette, as we are not responsible for any damage or losses.

Third-party services that have access to your data might also store data.

Guest Accounts

If you use Baguette under the "Guest" plan, any data you manually provide to Baguette (like names of bakery goods or customer names) could be publicly accessible to all other "Guest" users. Other "Guest" users may override your data at any time. The developer (kpoovakan) may delete your data at any time without prior notice.

Cookies

Yummy! Despite being an app for bakeries, Baguette does not use any digital cookies. Maybe you could use Baguette to bake physical cookies?

Please note that while Baguette does not directly use cookies, third-party services might use cookies.

Third Party Services

We may provide data to third-party services for the purpose of providing this service (Baguette) to you. Third-party services may have privacy policies that differ from ours. The third-party services that we use are Cloudflare, GitHub, and Katanya. Please note that Katanya is an Indonesian service, and its main documentation is in Indonesian (or, Bahasa Indonesia).

Data Rights

If you would like to remove our access to collecting your GitHub username, you must uninstall Baguette through your GitHub settings. If you would like to delete your manually entered data, please contact the developer (kpoovakan). We are currently working on a better system for data deletion.
